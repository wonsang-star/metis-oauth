Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

Purpose

Metis is a private personal AI assistant used only by its owner. It connects to Google services through OAuth to perform user-requested tasks.

Google User Data

Metis may access Gmail and Google Calendar data only after the user explicitly authorizes access through Google OAuth.

How Data Is Used

Google user data is used solely to provide personal assistant functions requested by the account owner, such as reading or organizing Gmail messages and reading, creating, or updating personal Calendar events within the granted permissions.

Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials and related application data may be stored locally on the owner's device only as needed to maintain the authorized connection.

Access Control

The application is intended for private use by the owner only.

Revoking Access

The user may revoke Metis access at any time through their Google Account security settings.

Contact

For questions about this application or this privacy policy, contact the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
